## 4. Findings

### 4.1 Resolution, not schedule, was the binding constraint on EfficientNet-B0

The 224 px grid produced 0.8185 / 0.8438 / 0.8506 across seeds — a 0.032 spread that looked like
seed noise. The 320 px retry produced 0.8680 / 0.8674 / 0.8648: **+0.029 mean and a seed sd that
collapses from 0.0169 to 0.0017**. The 224 px spread was therefore mostly optimisation variance
that a larger input removed, and the retry is the more trustworthy estimate of this
architecture's ability. G3 (0.90) remains missed by 0.033 on the single-seed mean and 0.021 on
the ensemble; the report should say so rather than round up.

This is the single documented deviation from the frozen protocol: one retry, EfficientNet-B0,
`ela` only, 320 px, 3 seeds, kept out of the McNemar grid.

### 4.2 Dual-stream fusion did not deliver the `rgb_ela` gain the design expected

| Architecture | `rgb` | `ela` | `rgb_ela` | `ela` − `rgb_ela` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| EfficientNet-B0 | 0.6709 | **0.8376** | 0.7264 | +0.111 |
| ResNet-18 + BiLSTM | 0.6662 | **0.8242** | 0.7415 | +0.083 |
| Swin-Tiny | 0.7432 | **0.8440** | 0.7992 | +0.045 |
| MLP (fusion inert) | **0.6128** | 0.4910 | 0.5995 | — |

`rgb_ela` never beat `ela`: the G2 claim fails in 9/9 seeds across the three architectures.
Two things in this table are worth the report's attention rather than a shrugged miss.

First, fusion does add over RGB alone — McNemar on `rgb` vs `rgb_ela` is significant after Holm
in all three architectures (§4.5) — but it recovers only part of what ELA alone already gives.
The `ela`-vs-`rgb_ela` gap shrinks as the fusion mechanism improves: 0.111 for EfficientNet-B0
and 0.083 for ResNet-18+BiLSTM, both of which use the design's `DualStem` (a pretrained RGB conv
**summed with** a freshly initialised 1-channel aux conv, `model_factory.DualStem`, no scale or
gate between them), versus 0.045 for Swin-Tiny, which concatenates the four input channels and
mixes them through token projection and attention.

Second, the obvious mechanical explanation for that ordering does not survive its own ablation.
If the summed stem with default initialisation were the cause, `--fusion concat` should close
the gap. It does not — it is marginally *worse* in both CNNs:

| Architecture | `rgb_ela` dual (3 seeds) | `rgb_ela` concat (3 seeds) | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| EfficientNet-B0 | 0.7264 ± 0.0109 | 0.7211 ± 0.0049 | −0.005 |
| ResNet-18 + BiLSTM | 0.7415 ± 0.0098 | 0.7308 ± 0.0067 | −0.011 |

So the fusion *mode* is not what limits `rgb_ela`; fusing a weaker RGB stream into a strong ELA
stream costs more than it adds on this dataset, however the fusion is done. The residual
architecture ordering (Swin fuses best) is real but unexplained by this ablation and is left as
an open observation rather than a claimed cause. The ablation was pre-sanctioned and is not part
of the frozen 36-run grid; its three-run arms are reported here and nowhere else.

Third, `ela` is the *worst* mode for the MLP (0.491) and the *best* mode for all three
convolutional/attention models. ELA is an edge-detector-like residual map: it needs spatial
filters to be useful, and it is the single most informative thing about this dataset. That is
consistent with §4.3's confound finding — ELA encodes both manipulation traces and lossless-vs-JPEG
history, and nothing in the grid separates the two.

The 320 px retry was not extended to `rgb_ela`, so every row here is 224 px.

### 4.3 The dominant confound is file format, not JPEG quality (G6)

`analysis/confound.py` measures two things on the test split: the quality-factor association and
a format-stratified macro-F1. On the 1,181 test images:

- **227 of 1,181 images carry no JPEG quantization table**: 223 of the 303 tampered images are
  TIFF and 4 authentic images are BMP, while the remaining 954 are JPEG. The classes are not
  format-matched, so a model can in principle read "is this a JPEG" instead of "is this forged".
- Among readable JPEGs (874 authentic vs 80 tampered): median quality **72 vs 85**,
  Mann-Whitney **p = 7.6e-43**. Quality is confounded with the label.
- Restricting to the JPEG-only slice (n = 954) drops macro-F1 by ≈ 0.11 for **every** input mode:

| Input mode | Full split | JPEG-only slice | Drop |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `ela` | 0.8377 | 0.7273 | 0.110 |
| `rgb_ela` | 0.7265 | 0.6077 | 0.119 |
| `rgb` | 0.6709 | 0.5614 | 0.110 |

The drop is uniform, so it is a property of that harder sub-population (80 tampered JPEGs), not
an ELA-specific shortcut — and it does **not** explain why `ela` beats `rgb_ela`. The same holds
in the other two architectures: `ela` falls 0.8242 → 0.7017 (ResNet) and 0.8440 → 0.7497 (Swin)
on the JPEG-only slice, and the mode ordering is unchanged in both. Two honest
consequences for the write-up: no absolute number in this report should be read as
format-independent generalization, and the ordering between input modes survives the
format control while the absolute level does not.

### 4.4 Grad-CAM is not localizing (G8 measured, not downgraded)

CASIA 2.0 does ship pixel masks (`CASIA 2 Groundtruth`, 5,123 files) in the same Kaggle dataset
as the images; the S0 readiness check reported them missing only because the split is reached
through a symlinked directory it does not descend into. Measured on `efficientnet_b0_ela_s3`
(the best 224 px run), test split, 295 of 303 tampered images with a mask:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Mean IoU | 0.1140 |
| Mean Dice | 0.1773 |
| Mean mask area | 0.079 of pixels |
| Mean predicted area | 0.214 of pixels |

An all-forged constant mask would score IoU 0.079 against these masks and a random mask of the
same 21% coverage about 0.06, so the CAM carries real but weak spatial signal — not a usable
localization output. The CAM grid at 224 px is 7 × 7 (one cell = 32 × 32 input pixels) and the
ELA stream's signal is diffuse, so this is the expected ceiling for this setup, not a bug. It is
reported descriptively; no localization claim enters the headline metrics.

### 4.5 Statistical comparison (G7)

Full tables in `docs/results/mcnemar.md`; every test uses each run's own stored decision
threshold and is scored on the test split, so selecting representatives on validation F1 never
touches the split being tested. Holm is applied within each pre-declared family and never pooled.

**Family A — input modes within an architecture (12 tests).** All pairs separate except one:
`ela` beats `rgb` (p_holm 1.2e-19 EffNet, 2.9e-31 ResNet, 5.7e-12 Swin) and beats `rgb_ela`
(1.7e-11, 1.3e-12, 0.036). `rgb_ela` beats `rgb` in all three (0.0011, 2.5e-12, 4.6e-08). The
exception is the MLP, where `ela` is indistinguishable from `rgb` (p_holm 0.111) because the MLP
cannot exploit ELA at all.

**Family B — architectures on their best mode (6 tests).**
- EfficientNet-B0 vs Swin-Tiny: **no significant difference** (b = 49, c = 40, p_holm 0.397).
- EfficientNet-B0 vs ResNet-18+BiLSTM: EffNet better (80 vs 31, p_holm 1.1e-05).
- ResNet-18+BiLSTM vs Swin-Tiny: Swin better (85 vs 45, p_holm 0.0011).
- MLP vs every CNN/transformer: far worse (p_holm ≤ 6.7e-37).

The headline consequence: the two best families (EffNet-B0 0.8376 and Swin-Tiny 0.8440) are
**statistically tied** despite Swin's higher mean — the 0.006 difference is seed noise. Any claim
that one of them is the better architecture on this split is unsupported.

Per-seed 95% bootstrap CIs are in `docs/results/<arch>.md`; they are the CIs of each reported
figure, not of the seed mean, and the McNemar tests above are the paired comparison the seed
means cannot provide on their own.

## 6. What the report should conclude

- The best honest single-run number is **0.8667** (EfficientNet-B0, `ela`, 320 px) and the best
  ensemble **0.8793**. Neither reaches the 0.90 target, and the 0.90 target should not be claimed.
- **ELA-only is the right input** on this dataset; adding RGB hurts at every fusion setting tried
  (dual and concat), and `ela` beats `rgb_ela` in 9/9 seeds with Holm-corrected McNemar support.
- **Swin-Tiny and EfficientNet-B0 are statistically tied**; ResNet-18+BiLSTM is significantly
  worse than both; the MLP is a deliberate lower bound, not a competitor.
- Every absolute number is format-dependent (§4.3): on the JPEG-only slice, macro-F1 drops ~0.11
  for every model. The report must state this instead of generalising.
- Localization (G8) is reported as measured and negative-ish (IoU 0.114); no localization claim
  enters the headline metrics.
